## B

**ballistic transverse thermoelectric effect** *(BTTE)* *n.* Transverse thermoelectric response in graphene nanostructures at room temperature and zero magnetic field, enabled by asymmetric scattering of ballistic carriers. Han et al., Nature Communications 3 Nov 2026: transverse Seebeck 27.9 μV K⁻¹, power factor ~35.8 μW cm⁻¹ K⁻². Voltage polarity retained on gradient reversal. *See also* graphene; thermoelectric. *Here* [[timeline/2026]].

## F

**ferroelectric orbital magnetism** *n.* Orbital magnetic order coupled to spontaneous electric polarization in rhombohedral hexalayer graphene. Deng, Xie, Li, Liu et al., Nature Physics 2026: sharp unipolar electric-field switching and electric-field-reversible magnetic hysteresis in the semimetallic regime. Distinct from near-zero-field multiferroicity. *See also* rhombohedral hexalayer graphene; orbital magnetism.

## R

**rhombohedral hexalayer graphene** *n.* Six graphene layers in rhombohedral (ABCABC…) stacking. Deng et al., Nature Physics 21 Oct 2026: in the semimetallic (overlapping band) regime, hosts correlated insulators, dual-carrier superconducting-like pockets, and multiferroic orbital magnetic orders including ferroelectric orbital magnetism. *See also* rhombohedral stacking; graphene. *Here* [[timeline/2026]].
