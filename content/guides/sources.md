## Graphene-Info and cousins

[Graphene-Info](https://www.graphene-info.com) (Metalgrass, since 2009) is the widest public radar for company names, SKU talk, and “this week” releases. OLED-Info and the rest of that network work the same way.

**Allowed**

- A *name* we did not have (plant, product mark, listing).
- A *date* that points at an ASX / AIM / SEDAR / Companies House / company PDF.
- A company-stated tonne or m² figure, logged as **company-stated** or **Preliminary**, with the year of the copy.

**Not allowed**

- Pasting their handbook, market-report tables, or catalog blurbs.
- Treating their paraphrase as the primary.
- A paywalled sentence reconstructed from memory.

If Graphene-Info is the only place a number appears, the row stays Preliminary until the PDF exists here. If their story and the filing disagree, the filing wins and we note the mismatch.
