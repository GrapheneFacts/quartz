## What the paper reports

Wang, Liao, Xu, Gao (Zhejiang) and Xu (Tsinghua), *Nature* (online 14 October 2026), doi:10.1038/s41586-026-11109-y.

Multilayer graphene oxide flakes are grown so that surface fold bands form. When cast into a film the folds clasp across layers. Reported laboratory figures:

- tensile strength 2.9 GPa
- toughness 84.5 MJ m⁻³
- elastic strain 5%

The authors link the triad to a shear-locking stress-transfer path that converts tensile force chains into compressive ones and suppresses interlayer slip.

## What it is not

Not a demonstration of aerospace components or armor. Laboratory film metrics. Independent replication and large-area data are not yet published. Single-crystal graphene remains stronger.
