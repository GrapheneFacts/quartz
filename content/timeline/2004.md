**Evidence:** Well-supported

Andre Geim, Konstantin Novoselov, and collaborators isolate few-layer and monolayer graphene by mechanical cleavage from graphite, transfer flakes to oxidized silicon, and report electric-field effect in atomically thin carbon films.

**Landmark paper:** Novoselov et al., *Science* **306**, 666 (2004).

Not the first time monolayer carbon was produced chemically, but the combination of clear monolayer identification, devices on an insulator, and electronic measurements triggered a sustained global research boom.
