**Evidence:** ISO cover date and Abraham paper. The vocabulary does not certify a bag of powder.

## Vocabulary

**ISO/TS 80004-13:2017** — first edition **September 2017** (ISO); IEC webstore date **14 November 2017**. 21 pages. Lists terms for graphene and related 2D materials, production methods, properties, characterization. Withdrawn when **80004-13:2024** replaced it. [[reference/iso-vocabulary]]

## Membranes

Abraham, Vasu, Williams, et al., *Nat. Nanotechnol.* **12**, 546 (2017). Physically confined GO laminates; interlayer *d* tuned ~**9.8 Å to 6.4 Å**. Ion permeation thermally activated; water less affected. Demo: **97% NaCl rejection** in that confined stack. Continuation of Nair 2012, not a municipal plant.

## Two columns

Prestige devices stay encapsulated heterostructures. Tonnes stay powder plants. Do not count Flagship “products” without opening the definition.
