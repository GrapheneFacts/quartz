**Evidence:** Well-supported for the landmark growth paper and for the existence of specialist media. GHz headlines are device demos, not shipping radios.

## Documented science / process

- Li, Cai, An, Kim, Nah, Yang, Piner, Velamakanni, Jung, Tutuc, Banerjee, Colombo, Ruoff, *Science* **324**, 1312 (2009) — large-area graphene grown by CVD on copper foils, a practical path to mostly monolayer film.
- IBM and academic groups report graphene FETs at high frequency on small devices.

## Industry / media

Graphene-Info and similar outlets begin the vendor-week rhythm that still feeds 2026 roundups. That is useful and also a bias toward whoever issues a PDF.

## Claims

Transparent electrodes and RF will “replace” incumbents. Replacement requires yield, price, and a band gap or a different circuit architecture — not demonstrated at scale this year.
