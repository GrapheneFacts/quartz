**Evidence:** Well-supported

P. R. Wallace calculates the electronic band structure of a **single layer of graphite** as a route to understanding bulk graphite. The linear dispersion near the Dirac points — now iconic for graphene — appears in this theoretical framework.

Wallace did not “discover graphene” as a lab material. He established a key theoretical foundation later generations tested on real monolayers.
