**Evidence:** Well-supported that COVID disrupted lab work and that GO-adjacent rumors begin circulating before they harden in 2021. Exact first-post dates vary by platform.

## Documented science / industry

- Device and Flagship work continues at reduced tempo. Masks, sensors, and antiviral-coating pitches appear; most are preliminary or opportunistic. Label each on its own source.

## Fringe / unverified

- Early pandemic content starts mixing *real* GO nanosafety papers with claims about vaccines, 5G, and “self-assembling” particles. The cluster is not yet at 2021 volume. See [[logs/conspiracies]] and [[timeline/2021]].

Bias rule for this year: a preprint on GO toxicity is not evidence of covert deployment. A viral video is not a methods section.
