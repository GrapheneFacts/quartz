**Evidence:** Well-supported historical consensus

**1986 (commonly cited):** Boehm, Setton, Stumpp and colleagues help fix **graphene** as the term for single graphite sheets.

Monolayer carbon is grown or observed on metal surfaces and on silicon carbide. These are often coupled to substrates — distinct from freely handled monolayers on SiO₂ used in the 2004 transport experiments.
