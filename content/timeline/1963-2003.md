**Evidence:** Well-supported historical consensus

## Naming (mid-1980s, often 1986)

Boehm, Setton, Stumpp and colleagues help fix **graphene** as the systematic name for a single graphite sheet (graphite + *-ene*). See [[timeline/1962]] and [[logs/theories]].

## Epitaxy and surface science (1970s onward)

Monolayer carbon is grown or observed on metal surfaces and produced by heating silicon carbide (Si sublimation). These layers are real and important. They are usually **stuck to a substrate**. That is a different object from a flake sitting on SiO₂ that you can gate like a transistor ([[timeline/2004]]).

## Theory weather

Textbook talk about whether strictly 2D crystals can exist (Mermin–Wagner context) shapes expectations. Pop histories oversell this as “physicists thought graphene was impossible.” The actual constraint is subtler. Log the *ideal*, not the myth: [[logs/theories]].

## Early-2000s industry

Nano graphene platelet / single-layer process filings show companies studying the material *before* the *Science* paper. That belongs on [[logs/companies]], not as proof that 2004 was unnecessary.
