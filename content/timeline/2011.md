**Evidence:** Well-supported for published device and film papers. “Ready for industry” language in speeches is aspiration.

## Documented

- High-frequency graphene transistors continue (IBM and others; frequencies in the 100 GHz class on short-gate demos).
- Roll-to-roll / large-area CVD transfers (the 30-inch class demonstration is 2010 *Nature Nanotechnology*, Bae et al., and is still the reference everyone cites in 2011 talks).
- National roadmaps proliferate. The EU FET Flagship shortlist that will become the Graphene Flagship is political fact in motion ([[timeline/2013]]).

## Tension worth keeping

Metre-scale *film* is not metre-scale *electronic-grade monolayer*. Optical demos and FET demos use different quality bars. Mixing them is how unbiased archive readers get misled.
