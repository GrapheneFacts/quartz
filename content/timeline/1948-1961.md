**Evidence:** Well-supported historical consensus

## 1948 — Ruess and Vogt

Transmission electron microscopy on dried graphene-oxide (graphite-oxide) suspensions shows very thin, creased carbon films. Thickness is in the nanometre class. This is imaging of the oxide route, not a Hall bar.

## 1950s

Reduction chemistry, ever-thinner flakes, and arguments about how few layers you are actually looking at. The conversation is colloid and surface carbon, sitting next to growing graphite-moderator and electrode industries.

## Why the decade matters

Without this imaging-and-oxide work, [[timeline/1962|Boehm 1962]] has no runway. Without Wallace ([[timeline/1947]]), later device people have no Dirac language. The two threads are still separate.
