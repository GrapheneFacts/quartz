**Evidence:** Well-supported historical consensus

## 1948 — Ruess & Vogt

Transmission electron microscopy on dried graphene-oxide suspensions shows very thin, creased carbon films (nanometre-scale thickness).

## 1950s–early 1960s

Continued work on graphite oxide, reduction chemistry, and ever-thinner flakes — setting up Boehm’s monolayer reports.
