**Evidence:** Well-supported device papers. Market slides are forecasts.

## Vertical heterostructure FET

Britnell L. et al., *Science* **335**, 947–950 (2012). doi:10.1126/science.1218461. Graphene / thin hBN or MoS₂ / graphene. Room-temperature on/off about **50** (hBN barrier) and about **10,000** (MoS₂ barrier) in that prototype. A gap-work-around, not CMOS.

## Lateral graphene / hBN

Levendorf, Kim, Brown et al., *Nature* **488**, 627–632 (2012). Patterned regrowth of graphene and hBN in the same sheet. Circuit-language demo, still a lab film.

## Theme

Van der Waals stacks treated as the device (continuous with Dean 2010). Reviews: no gap, no low-power CMOS twin. Honest near paths: RF, sensors, photonics, membranes, additives.
