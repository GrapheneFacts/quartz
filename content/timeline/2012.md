**Evidence:** Well-supported for stacked 2D devices as a research theme. Market forecasts this year are not measurements.

## Documented science

- Graphene / hBN stacks and other van der Waals heterostructures show that the interesting device may be a *stack*, not a standalone graphene FET fighting silicon on silicon’s terms.
- Mobility on boron nitride versus SiO₂ becomes a standard comparison.

## Policy / money

Flagship competition language hardens. Consultancies publish billion-dollar graphene market graphs. Those graphs are not primary sources; file them as forecasts.

## Open

Contacts, band gap (or lack of one), contamination from transfer polymer (PMMA and relatives).
