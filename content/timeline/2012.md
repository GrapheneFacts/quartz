**Evidence:** Well-supported as a research-theme year. Market-size PDFs are forecasts.

## Science

Van der Waals heterostructures — graphene plus hBN plus (soon) TMDCs — are treated as the device, not a lone FET on SiO₂. Encapsulated stacks raise mobility and cut substrate disorder. This is continuous with Dean et al. 2010 ([[timeline/2010]]), now a field style.

Reviews start saying the quiet part: without a band gap you do not build a low-power digital switch that looks like CMOS. RF, sensors, photonics, membranes, and additives are the honest near paths.

## Money and language

Consultancies publish billion-dollar “graphene market 2020” charts. File as forecast. Flagship selection politics continue; the Commission decision lands in January 2013.

## Industry texture

Powder/platelet capacity announcements vs CVD-film pilots vs SiC wafers are three businesses. Coverage that uses one word for all three is the bias this archive is trying not to copy.

## Open

Contacts, transfer contamination (PMMA and cousins), grain boundaries on Cu CVD, and a definition of “graphene” a purchasing agent can put in a contract (ISO vocabulary is still years out: [[timeline/2017]]).
