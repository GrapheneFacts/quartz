**Evidence:** Well-supported (program-level)

CVD graphene on copper and transfer methods scale in labs. Flexible electronics, RF transistors, and photonics demos multiply. Policy discussions in the EU toward a large coordinated initiative.

High expectations (“graphene will replace silicon”) mixed with sober engineering constraints still shape industry communication today.
