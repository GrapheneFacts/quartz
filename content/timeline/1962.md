**Evidence:** Well-supported (with later historiographic discussion)

Hanns-Peter Boehm and colleagues report extremely thin carbon films from chemical reduction of graphite oxide, including identification of **monolayer** flakes.

The 2004 Manchester contribution is usually framed as *unambiguous isolation, identification on insulating substrates, and electronic measurement* that launched the modern field.

The word **graphene** is associated with Boehm and co-authors in the mid-1980s (often cited as 1986).
