**Evidence:** Well-supported

The European Commission launches the **Graphene Flagship** (with the Human Brain Project) as a FET Flagship: long-horizon coordination across universities and companies, roadmaps, standards talk, pilot lines.

This is the cleanest single row on [[logs/companies]] for a *public program start*. Original core phase is often described through ~2023, with follow-on under Horizon Europe. Treat patent-count press releases as marketing unless you open the filings.
