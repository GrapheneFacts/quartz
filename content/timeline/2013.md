**Evidence:** Commission / Chalmers releases for the Flagship. AIM notice for AGM.

## Flagship dates (do not collapse)

- **28 January 2013:** Commission names Graphene a FET Flagship (with the Human Brain Project). Chalmers coordinator **Jari Kinaret**. Initial ~30-month EC budget **€54 million**. Official tag: GRAPHENE 604391.
- **10–11 October 2013:** kickoff at Chalmers. Ramp-up **1 Oct 2013 – 31 Mar 2016**. €1 billion over ten years is a *programme class*, not a 2013 deposit.

Advisory names in the January release: Geim (chair), Fert, von Klitzing, Novoselov; industry voices Nokia, Airbus.

Kinaret: the project will not try to “compete with Korea on graphene screens.”

## Listing

**Applied Graphene Materials** AIM IPO this year (Durham spin-out). First widely called graphene IPO in UK copy. [[topics/finance/history]] · [[logs/companies]]

## What the Flagship is not

A single European company. A guarantee of 2023 SKUs. A veto on Korean or US CVD labs.
