**Evidence:** Well-supported as a European Commission FET Flagship start. Delivery metrics over the next decade must be read in reports and filings, not launch brochures.

## Documented

- Graphene Flagship begins alongside the Human Brain Project. Budget class is billion-euro over a long horizon, split across many partners.
- Scope includes graphene *and related 2D materials*, standards, pilots, and industry partners — explicitly not only Manchester-style flakes.

## Parallel facts, same year

Labs keep publishing oxide, CVD, and SiC papers. Startups keep selling platelets. None of that starts *because* of the Flagship press conference, and none of it is disproved by it.
