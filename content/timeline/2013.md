**Evidence:** Well-supported from Commission / Chalmers / Flagship releases. €1 billion headline ≠ cash in 2013.

## Dates (do not collapse them)

- **28 January 2013:** European Commission names Graphene one of the first FET Flagships (with the Human Brain Project). Coordinated at **Chalmers**, director **Jari Kinaret**. Launch releases cite ~76–126 groups depending on how partners are counted, 17 countries, initial ~30-month EC budget **€54 million**.
- Official project name in Commission paperwork: GRAPHENE — Graphene-Based Revolutions in ICT and Beyond (604391).
- **10–11 October 2013:** kickoff ceremony at Chalmers (Gothenburg). Ramp-up phase **1 October 2013 – 31 March 2016**, EC €54 million. Steady-state talk under Horizon 2020 from April 2016, with *expected* ~€50 million/year EC — plus partner matching. Commentators immediately note that €1 billion over ten years is a *programme class*, not a cheque deposited in 2013.

Advisory names in the January release: Geim (chair), Fert, von Klitzing, Novoselov; industry voices from Nokia and Airbus.

Kinaret on scope (Lancaster / Flagship quotes): the project will not try to “compete with Korea on graphene screens”; production methods are in-scope.

## What the Flagship is

A coordination machine: work packages, open calls for extra engineering groups, roadmaps for ICT, energy, sensors, composites. Related layered materials are in the mandate, not only honeycomb carbon.

## What it is not

A single European graphene company. A guarantee that 2023 products will exist. A refutation of Korean or US CVD work. Labs and platelet vendors outside the consortium keep publishing and selling without waiting for Gothenburg.

## Same year, other tracks

Oxide, CVD, SiC, and LPE papers continue. Device groups keep quoting f_T. None of that starts or stops because of the sail-raising photo.
