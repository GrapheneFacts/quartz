**Evidence:** Fringe for the covert-ingredient claim. Well-supported that regulator and manufacturer lists for licensed COVID vaccines do not name graphene oxide.

## What is documented

- Microscopy-of-a-droplet and petition-hearing material circulate widely. Some images are real images of *something*; chain of custody to an authenticated vial of a named licensed product is what is missing.
- GO *is* used in some laboratory imaging of spike protein (cryo-EM grids, etc.). That is not a formulation ingredient.
- Experimental GO-adjuvant work in animals exists in the literature. That is not a licensed human product composition.
