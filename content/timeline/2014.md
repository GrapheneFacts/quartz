## Photodetectors

- Liu, Chang, Liu, Zhong, *Nat. Nanotechnol.* **9**, 273 (2014). Double-layer graphene heterostructure phototransistor. Room-temperature visible-to-mid-IR; mid-IR responsivity **>1 A/W** in that report.
- Koppens, Mueller, Avouris, Ferrari, Vitiello, Polini, *Nat. Nanotechnol.* **9**, 780 (2014). Review of graphene / 2D / hybrid photodetectors — the field’s map after the 2013 waveguide cluster.
- Cai, et al., *Nat. Nanotechnol.* (2014) photothermoelectric THz detector: room-temperature THz class, speed claim vs then-existing room-temp THz parts. Still a lab detector.

These sit with 2013 waveguide PDs. None is a catalogue SKU.
