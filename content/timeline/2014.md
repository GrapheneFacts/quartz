**Evidence:** Well-supported as a program year. Individual product launches need their own primary notes.

## Documented

- Flagship work packages publish roadmaps (energy, composites, electronics, biomedical). Roadmaps are plans, not shipments.
- More labs quote sheet resistance and Raman I(2D)/I(G) in the same paragraph as “graphene.” That pairing is progress; it is still not a legal definition.

## Commercial weather

Sports gear, coatings, and battery-additive pitches increase. Price-per-kg figures circulate without saying *which* grade (GO, rGO, few-layer powder, CVD film). An unbiased archive stores the number *and* the missing grade.
