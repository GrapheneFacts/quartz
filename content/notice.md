This is the only official disclaimer and transparency page for graphenefacts.org. Other pages should not repeat it. Sources used to build pages: [[citations|Citations]].

## What this site is

A public, labeled archive of graphene-family materials: forms, history, papers, plants, money notices, occupational and biomedical records, and (separately) circulating rumors. Short notes may still appear on [@GrapheneFacts](https://x.com/GrapheneFacts). The durable copy is here.

Files: [graphene-archive](https://github.com/GrapheneFacts/graphene-archive). Engine: [quartz](https://github.com/GrapheneFacts/quartz) (`content/` on `v5`). Private compile work is not published.

Mail: [contact@graphenefacts.org](mailto:contact@graphenefacts.org).

## What this site is not

Not medical advice. Not investment advice. Not legal advice. Not a recommendation to buy, sell, hold, inhale, implant, or ingest anything. Not a newsletter, handbook shop, or competitor to trade portals.

## How confidence is marked

Folder is for browsing. The **label** on a claim is for confidence.

| Label | Means |
|-------|--------|
| Well-supported | Peer-reviewed paper, regulator text, or confirmed order |
| Preliminary | Preprint, company lab, pilot, or company-stated tonnes |
| Unverified / Unsourced | No solid primary |
| Fringe | Plot-style narrative, documented only |

Unsourced is not automatically Fringe. Detail: [[guides/evidence-labels]]. Trade portals are radar, not the shelf: [[guides/sources]]. Figures: [[topics/finance/how-we-log-figures]].

## Corrections

Send a source to the mail above. A later filing that retracts a number stays on the page with the retraction. It is not deleted to tidy the story.
