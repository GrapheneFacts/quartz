**Evidence:** Well-supported occupational data; 10 µg/m³ is a band, not an OSHA PEL

NIOSH sampled workers at 11 US graphene producers/users. Many samples exceeded a conservative occupational exposure band of **10 µg/m³** when dry powder was handled. Recommendation: supply graphene in masterbatch/liquid rather than loose powder. No reported human disease cluster tied to these data.
