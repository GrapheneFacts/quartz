**Not medical advice.** Graphene-family materials are not one toxicology object. GO ≠ cleaved monolayer ≠ dry nanoplatelet powder. [[reference/forms]] · [[reference/safety]]

## Humans

| What | What the record supports | Label |
|------|--------------------------|-------|
| Worker disease cluster at graphene plants | Reviews used by NIOSH still report **no known case series** of adverse effects in GFN workers | Absence of reports ≠ lifelong proof |
| Controlled GO inhalation (Andrews et al. 2024; often tied to **NCT03659864**) | ~14 healthy volunteers; ~**2 h** at ~**200 µg/m³** thin purified GO; no acute change in heart rate, BP, spirometry, or the inflammatory markers they tracked | Preliminary; one dose; hours of follow-up; larger sheets excluded |
| Graphene cortical electrodes (**NCT06368310**, INBRAIN) | First-in-human intraoperative use; early 2025 writeups of a small set: usable signal, no device-linked serious events *in that set* | Preliminary *device* data |
| GO as a licensed drug / vaccine ingredient | 2026-era reviews: no Phase I/II GO *therapeutic* in that sense; licensed shot lists do not include GO | Well-supported *as a negative* for the slogan |

## Animals and cells

High-dose **lung** protocols in rodents can inflame and, in some designs, trend fibrotic. Outcome tracks lateral size, thickness, oxidation, metal leftovers, and whether the aerosol is respirable. Skin uptake in banding reviews looks limited. Gut and IV are other piles. A cell-culture IC50 is not an occupational limit.

## Workplaces

NIOSH field work (44 workers, 11 US sites, elemental carbon):

- Respirable: about **&lt;0.01–1,825 µg/m³**
- Inhalable: about **0.01–6,327 µg/m³**
- Geometric means much lower than the maxima
- Dry-powder **primary** plants higher than shops that only handle masterbatch or pellets
- A large share of samples sat above a conservative **&lt;10 µg/m³** *band* — **not an OSHA PEL**

Same literature: NIOSH banding case study landed graphene-family materials in **band E** (treat as potent at low air concentration). Method is conservative by design. Other papers float different draft guidance values for platelets vs oxide; they do not collapse to one number.
