**Not medical advice.** Pair with [[topics/health/effects]] and [[reference/safety]].

## Dry GNP / nanoplatelet powder — workplace air

- **Primary:** NIOSH field work in US graphene plants. Respirable elemental carbon from below **0.01 µg/m³** to about **1,825 µg/m³**. Conservative evaluation band often cited near **<10 µg/m³** (not an OSHA PEL). Many dry-powder samples sat above that band.
- **Label:** Well-supported *as hygiene measurements*. Not a human disease study.
- [[topics/policy/2026-09-16-niosh-graphene-exposure]]

## Thin purified GO aerosol — short controlled human inhalation

- **Primary:** Andrews JPM et al. *Nat. Nanotechnol.* **19**, 705–714 (2024). doi:10.1038/s41565-023-01572-3. **NCT03659864**.
- **What they did:** 14 young healthy volunteers; double-blind randomized visits; small and ultrasmall GO vs filtered air; **200 µg/m³** for **2 h**.
- **What they reported:** exposure tolerated; heart rate, blood pressure, lung function, inflammatory markers not changed by size in that window; mild signal in an *ex vivo* thrombus model; authors: no overt acute cardiorespiratory harm in this design.
- **Label:** Preliminary *human, one acute protocol*. Not chronic shop air. Not GNP powder.

## GrapheneBlack-class powder — guideline tox package (not a volunteer trial)

- **Primary trail:** company-commissioned package on GrapheneBlack 3X summarized in open literature (e.g. 2020 crystals paper discussing Charles River work): dermal, acute inhalation, genotox endpoints with no adverse finding at the doses they ran; inhalation NOAEL language at the maximum achieved aerosol they could generate.
- **Label:** Preliminary *product-specific animal/in vitro package*. Not NIOSH field air. Not Andrews GO.

## GO / rGO — rodent lung, high dose

- **Primary pile:** Graphene Flagship toxicology stream, collected in Fadeel et al. *ACS Nano* **12**, 10582–10620 (2018) and the 2025 Flagship lessons paper Fadeel et al. *Small* **21**, 2404570 (2025), PMC11840464.
- Flagship takeaway they print: GBMs are not one material; they did not find “excessive or alarming” toxicity in the tested set; realistic life-cycle exposure still thin.
- Older high-dose rodent lung papers still show inflammation and, in some protocols, fibrotic trend — size, metals, oxidation decide which paper you are holding.
- **Label:** Preliminary / mixed animal. Cite the specific paper, not “graphene.”

## Skin

- Flagship / banding reviews: limited uptake for the thin GO sheets they tested; macrophages can internalize thin GO without the “blade cutting the cell” slogan holding up in later Flagship work (Fadeel 2025 discussion of earlier membrane-piercing citations).
- GrapheneBlack 3X dermal package: no adverse finding at tested doses (company-commissioned trail above).
- **Label:** Preliminary. Gloves in a powder shop are still hygiene, not a fashion choice.

## Swallowed or IV “GO medicine”

- No Phase I/II therapeutic of raw GO as a licensed drug in 2026-era reviews we log.
- **Label:** Well-supported *as a negative for the slogan*. Delivery-vehicle papers are a different object — name the construct.

## rGO / graphene film on an electrode — intraoperative cortex

- **Registry:** NCT06368310. INBRAIN Graphene Cortical Interface during tumor resection. Manchester / Northern Care Alliance. Actual start 6 Aug 2024.
- **Interim company/meeting trail (first 4 patients):** no device-related adverse events in that set; high-gamma / phoneme-linked activity claimed during awake mapping. Meeting abstract: Coope et al., SURG-92 style interim.
- **Label:** Preliminary *device*. Not powder toxicology. [[topics/health/biomedical-catalogue]]

## Composite in a tire, paint, or shirt

- Not evidence of monolayer in blood.
- **Label:** Category error if used that way. [[logs/unsourced]]

## Licensed vaccine ingredient lists

- Do not include GO.
- **Label:** Well-supported *as a negative*. Plot versions: [[logs/conspiracies]] · [[topics/fringe/2026-09-16-go-vaccine-5g-claims]]
