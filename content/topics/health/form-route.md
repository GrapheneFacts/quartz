## Matrix

**Dry nanoplatelet / GNP powder, inhaled at work**
NIOSH field samples at US plants. Respirable elemental carbon from below 0.01 µg/m³ to about 1,825 µg/m³. Conservative band **<10 µg/m³** is not an OSHA PEL. Many dry-powder samples sat above it. Label: well-supported *as field hygiene*. [[topics/policy/2026-09-16-niosh-graphene-exposure]]

**Thin purified GO aerosol, short controlled human inhalation**
Andrews et al. 2024; often tied to **NCT03659864**. About 2 hours at about 200 µg/m³ in healthy volunteers. No acute change in the endpoints they tracked. One dose, hours of follow-up. Label: Preliminary human.

**GO / rGO, rodent lung, high dose**
Reviews collect inflammation and, in some protocols, fibrotic trend. Size, metals, and oxidation decide the picture. Label: Preliminary / mixed animal pile.

**GO / rGO, skin**
Banding reviews: limited uptake. Not a license to skip gloves in a powder shop. Label: Preliminary.

**GO as a swallowed or IV “medicine”**
2026-era reviews: no Phase I/II GO therapeutic in that sense. Label: well-supported *as a negative for the slogan*.

**CVD / transferred film on an electrode, intraoperative**
INBRAIN **NCT06368310**. Early small-set writeups: usable signal, no device-linked serious events in that set. Label: Preliminary *device*. Not powder toxicology. [[topics/health/biomedical-catalogue]]

**Composite part in a consumer good**
Not evidence of monolayer in blood. Label: category error if used that way. [[logs/unsourced]]

**Licensed vaccine ingredient lists**
Do not include GO. Label: well-supported *as a negative*. Plot versions: [[logs/conspiracies]]

## Still thin

Chronic low-dose human inhalation. Gut and IV fate by exact grade. Occupational clusters. Those absences are data gaps, not slogans.
