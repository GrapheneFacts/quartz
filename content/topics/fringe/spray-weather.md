## What circulates

- Aircraft contrails recast as “chemtrails” that contain graphene or GO.
- Weather-control or solar-geoengineering programmes said to spray graphene as the payload.
- The same payload then tied to 5G, vaccines, or livestock deaths in one thread.

## What the public record actually holds

- Graphene-family powders are made in plants and labs. That is [[topics/manufacturing/directory]].
- Occupational air samples exist at some of those plants. That is [[topics/health/effects]] (NIOSH field notes).
- There is no regulator text on this shelf that treats graphene as an authorized aerial-spray climate tool.
- A toxicity paper in rodents is not a flight log. [[reference/safety]]

## Why it stays on Fringe

The jump is always the same: a real material + a real high-dose paper + a photograph of a sky → a programme. The middle step is missing. Missing citation = [[logs/unsourced]]. A cover-up story = this column.
