## What leaves the plant

G+ = Directa’s pristine GNP family (plasma super-expansion of graphite, then water exfoliation in their process copy). Grades they name: MICRO/PURE G+ powders, PASTE/LIQUID dispersions, GRAFYSORBER super-expanded graphite.

## Textiles and membranes

- G+ **Planar Thermal Circuit** — printed circuit on fabric; heat-spread / IR story in company and Techtextil copy.
- Padding/impregnation into viscose, cotton, denim — company: thermal, antistatic, antimicrobial language. Antimicrobial slogans are **company claims**, not a drug monograph here.
- Membranes: GRAFYTHERM (G+ in ~20 µm water-based PU), GRAFYGUARD (that PU + PTFE), GRAFYOHM (conductive membrane). DTS acquisition 2016 in company copy.

## GRAFYSORBER

Barriers, pillows, socks: fabric shell filled with super-expanded graphite. Company: absorb ~**100×** its weight, oil not water, press to recover hydrocarbon. Spill kit, not a graphene battery.

Skin-safe / non-cytotoxic certificates belong on a certificate row when the PDF is attached.
