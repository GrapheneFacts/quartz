## Established enough to teach

- Monolayer isolation + field effect (2004). [[timeline/2004]]
- Dirac / QHE (2005). [[timeline/2005]]
- Intrinsic mechanical / optical numbers on *good flakes*. [[reference/properties]]
- Cu CVD transferable film (2009). [[timeline/2009]]
- GHz FETs on SiC and CVD (2010–11). Real f_T; not a phone SoC. [[timeline/2010]] · [[timeline/2011]]
- Encapsulated transport on hBN (Dean 2010). [[timeline/2010]]
- Vertical tunneling FET: Britnell et al. *Science* **335**, 947 (2012). On/off ~50 (hBN) / ~10,000 (MoS₂) in that prototype. [[timeline/2012]]
- Lateral graphene/hBN circuitry: Levendorf et al. *Nature* **488**, 627 (2012). [[timeline/2012]]
- Magic-angle bilayer superconductivity / correlated states (2018). Different object than powder. [[timeline/2018]]
- ISO vocabulary: 80004-13 **2017** and **2024**; powder/dispersion structure 21356-1 **2021**. [[reference/iso-vocabulary]]
- Water / ion stories at vacancies and holes. [[topics/research/2026-09-10-water-dissociation-graphene-vacancy]]
- LPE can sort few-layer material. [[topics/research/2026-09-12-few-layer-graphene-centrifugation]]

## Projected in 2004–12 that did not ship as sold

- Room-temperature digital logic replacing CMOS at scale.
- ITO replacement “tomorrow” after the 30-inch film demo.
- “Graphene battery” as one chemistry.

- Grade metrology cheap enough for a PO.
- Long-term biological fate of each *form*.
