## Established enough to teach

- 2004 isolation + field effect. [[timeline/2004]]
- 2005 Dirac / QHE. [[timeline/2005]]
- Cu CVD transferable film (2009). [[timeline/2009]]
- GHz FETs 2010–11. [[timeline/2010]] · [[timeline/2011]]
- Encapsulated transport on hBN (Dean 2010).
- Britnell 2012 vertical tunnel FET. [[timeline/2012]]
- Levendorf 2012 lateral graphene/hBN.
- Furchi 2012 microcavity PD (~21 mA/W in that cavity).
- Nair et al. 2012 GO laminates: helium-tight, water fast. [[timeline/2012]]
- Koenig / Bunch 2012 etched-pore graphene sieve.
- 2013 waveguide graphene PDs (*Nat. Photonics* cluster): >0.1 A/W, >20 GHz class demos. [[timeline/2013]]
- Magic-angle bilayer (2018). [[timeline/2018]]
- ISO 80004-13 **2017/2024**; 21356-1 **2021**. [[reference/iso-vocabulary]]

## Projected in 2004–12 that did not ship as sold

- CMOS replacement at scale.
- ITO replacement “tomorrow.”
- One “graphene battery.”

## Still open

- Cheap grade metrology for a PO.
- Form-by-form biological fate.
- Twisted stacks as a platform, not a SKU.
