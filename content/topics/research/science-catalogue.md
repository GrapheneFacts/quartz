- 2004 isolation + field effect. [[timeline/2004]]

- GHz FETs 2010–11. [[timeline/2010]] · [[timeline/2011]]

- Furchi 2012 microcavity PD.
- Nair 2012 GO water/helium membranes.
- Koenig/Bunch 2012 etched-pore sieve.
- 2013 waveguide PDs (*Nat. Photonics*). [[timeline/2013]]

- Cai et al. 2014 room-temperature graphene THz photothermoelectric detector.
- Magic-angle bilayer (2018).
- ISO 80004-13 2017/2024; 21356-1 2021. [[reference/iso-vocabulary]]
