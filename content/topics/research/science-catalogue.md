## Projected in 2004–12 that did not ship as sold

- Room-temperature digital logic replacing CMOS at scale.
- ITO replacement “tomorrow” after the 30-inch film demo.
- “Graphene battery” as one chemistry.

Those are logged as theory weather and product rhetoric. [[logs/theories]] · [[reference/applications]] · [[logs/unsourced]]

## Still open science (not fringe)

- Defect-by-defect chemistry that is reproducible off one group’s reactor.
- Grade metrology cheap enough for a PO. [[reference/standards]]
- Long-term biological fate of each *form*.
- Twisted stacks and heterostructures as a materials platform (working, not a consumer SKU).
