## Did not ship as sold

These were the 2004–12 public pitch. The papers above are real. The *products* named in that pitch did not arrive as advertised.

- **Replace silicon CMOS.** Graphene has no usable gap in the simple sheet. Foundry economics never moved. [[logs/theories]] · [[timeline/2012]]
- **Replace ITO tomorrow.** Large CVD films exist. Touch-panel qualification, yield, and transfer damage are the rest of the work. [[timeline/2010]] · [[reference/applications]]
- **The graphene battery.** Always unpack: conductive additive vs anode vs coating vs a new cell chemistry. GMG 10C small-cell talk stays Preliminary. [[topics/industry/2026-09-09-gmg-battery-cycling]] · [[reference/applications]]

## Still open

- **Cheap PO-grade metrology.** Plants sell powder and film under one word. ISO 21356-1 exists; incoming-lot tools that a buyer can run without a national lab are still thin. [[reference/standards]] · [[reference/iso-vocabulary]]
- **Form-by-form biological fate.** GO ≠ rGO ≠ dry GNP ≠ a peeled monolayer. Clearance, dose, and long follow-up are not one number. [[topics/medical/biology]] · [[topics/health/form-route]]
- **Twisted stacks as a platform, not a SKU.** MATBG / MATTG / TDBG are measurement platforms. They are not a catalogue part. [[timeline/2018]] · [[timeline/2021]] · [[timeline/2023]] · [[guides/glossary]]
