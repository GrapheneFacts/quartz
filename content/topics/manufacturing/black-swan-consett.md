## What it is

Black Swan Graphene Inc. (TSXV **SWAN**, OTCQX BSWGF). Formed 2021 from Mason Graphite + Thomas Swan IP in company copy. Toronto office; **production at Thomas Swan, Consett, United Kingdom**.

Process language: patented processing / high-pressure homogenization (GEA Ariete class in the 2025 expansion notice) — platelet / dispersion family, not a Cu-CVD mill.

## Capacity the company states

- Pre-expansion: up to **40 t/year**.
- **3 Jun 2025** order: next unit to take the Consett line to **140 t/year**. Graphene-Info later (Jul 2025) says that expansion completed.
- 2023 scoping study talk of a much larger Québec plant (MD&A cites an initial target near **9,868 t/year**) is a *study*, not a built mill. Do not file Québec as operating.

## How to read it

40 → 140 t at Consett is a company scale-up at an existing UK site. It is not 4,000 t GrapheneBlack and not a 2004 flake foundry.
