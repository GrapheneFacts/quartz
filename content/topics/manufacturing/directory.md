## Feedstock (“harvest”)

Nobody harvests monolayer graphene from a field. Rows that belong under feedstock:

- Natural and synthetic **graphite** mines and processors (Talga and others are *graphite-to-anode* names that get pulled into graphene lists — say so).
- Oxide route: graphite → graphite oxide → GO / rGO. [[reference/production]]
- LPE: sonication / shear + centrifuge. [[topics/research/2026-09-12-few-layer-graphene-centrifugation]]
- Bottom-up CVD from methane (or other C gases).
