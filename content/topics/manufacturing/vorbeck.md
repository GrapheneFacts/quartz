## Site

Vorbeck Materials Corp. **8306 Patuxent Range Rd, Ste 105, Jessup, MD 20794.** Vor-x / Vor-ink graphene formulations in long-standing trade copy.

## Why it stays on the list

DoD SBIR Phase II W911QX22C0019 (2022, option 2023) — graphene-based composite EMI shielding — still lists that Jessup address. Early US name that did not vanish. Conductive ink / composite house, not GrapheneBlack scale.
