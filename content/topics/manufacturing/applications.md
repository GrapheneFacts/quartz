## What plants sell into

Most tonnes leave as **powder, paste, masterbatch, or GO**. Most square metres leave as **CVD film or coating**. Appliances (a heater, a filter, a coated coil) are downstream of those two streams.

### Composites and elastomers

GNP / FLG in PU, epoxy, rubber, concrete, asphalt. Job is usually modulus, wear, barrier, or a fire/char story at low wt%. Example buyer: [[topics/manufacturing/pacific-urethanes]]. Cementene-class concrete was Versarien language — historical. [[topics/manufacturing/versarien]]

### Coatings and inks

Anti-corrosion, thermal-rebuild on HVAC coils (GMG THERMAL-XR salt-spray talk), EMI paints, Vor-ink. [[topics/manufacturing/gmg]] · [[topics/manufacturing/vorbeck]]

### Energy-storage additives

Conductive carbon in a cathode or anode slurry. Ask loading and whether acetylene black would have done. Graphene-*monoxide* anode additive is a different molecule. [[topics/manufacturing/conovate]]

### Films and heaters

CVD or R2R film → touch, EMI, transparent conductor pilots, **resistive heaters** (Charmgraphene heaters; 2D Carbon heater-film language). An appliance here is a mat, tape, or panel — not a graphene toaster SKU from Manchester.

### Media and devices

ZenGUARD on a mask or HVAC filter. INBRAIN is an investigational electrode, not a mill product. [[topics/manufacturing/zentek]]

### Tools
