## The Sixth Element (Changzhou)

- Founded 2011. Changzhou, Jiangsu.
- GO, rGO, graphene powder, some CVD film language.
- IDTechEx / company trail: **400 t/year** class, with older copy talking **1,000 t/year** after a 2020 expansion. Treat both as company/analyst stated.
- REACH consortium founding member in graphene-industry copy.
- First Graphene has discussed a China commercial path that names this firm as a partner in 2026 trade notes — partnership ≠ a second FGR mill.

## Chongqing Graphene / Moxi-class CVD

- Established 2013 in trade profiles (Shanghai Nanjiang + Chongqing Institute JV language).
- CVD sheets and touch-panel films.
- Company/trade: up to **3 million sheets/year** without a published sheet size — Unverified as tonnes.
- 2018 Evergrande stake language in Graphene-Info (~US$21.4 million for 35%). Secondary.

- Appears on single-crystal / film producer lists. Confirm the current SKU before quoting m².

## XFNANO (Nanjing-class vendor)

- Research-bottle and powder catalogue used in methods sections worldwide. Lot size ≠ plant rating.

## Graphene Square (Korea)

- CVD films. Academic and vendor SKUs. Not a 4,000 t powder mill.

## Charmgraphene / Knano / LeaderNano

- Recurring names in 2024–2026 market-report tables. Need a plant address + a year’s document before a dedicated page.

## How a name graduates

Named street, process, and a primary (filing, paper methods, or customs). Until then it stays on this list.
