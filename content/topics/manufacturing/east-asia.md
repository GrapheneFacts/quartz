## The Sixth Element (Changzhou)

- Legal name: 常州第六元素材料科技股份有限公司 / The Sixth Element (Changzhou) Materials Technology Co., Ltd.
- Founded **November 2011** (company about page). NEEQ **831190** from **15 Oct 2014** in Chinese company directories.
- **Address on the current site footer:** 60 Jinping Road, Wujin, Changzhou, Jiangsu (江苏省常州市武进区锦平路60号), postcode 213161. Older directory rows also list West Taihu Avenue 9 / plant 8.
- **Company-stated capacity (homepage / about, retrieved 2026):** graphene oxide **1,100 t/year**; graphene powder **150 t/year**. About-page timeline: 1 t GO pilot 2012; 100 t GO line 2013; Nantong phase-1 2020 “GO to 1,000 t/year.”
- English 2020 plant note (c6th.com): Nantong first phase **500 t/a GO + 150 t/a graphene**, group talk of **1,000 t/a** total after that phase. Those are company pages, not customs audits.
- REACH registration language in the same English note.
- Partner talk with First Graphene in 2026 trade notes ≠ a second FGR mill.
