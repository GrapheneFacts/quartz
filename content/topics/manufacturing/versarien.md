## What it was

Versarien plc, Longhope Business Park, Monmouth Road, Gloucestershire (Units 1A–D). AIM from **2013**. Graphene admixture language included **Cementene**. Acquired Cambridge Graphene and 2-DTech in the mid-2010s in company/trade copy. Cheltenham Chosen View Road also appears as an office address.

## Status (do not list as operating)

- **Jul 2025:** company said it could not sell certain UK technology assets; expected Versarien Graphene Ltd into administration; Cambridge Graphene Ltd and 2-DTech Ltd toward voluntary liquidation; Korea ops ceased (Graphene-Info recap of the RNS).
- Companies House: Versarien Graphene Limited (07475659) — administrator appointed **Aug 2025**; registered office moved to Leonard Curtis, Manchester.
- **6 Jan 2026:** Versarien plc joint administrators Andrew Knowles and Andrew Poxon (Leonard Curtis). Shares had been suspended. Remaining assets in that notice: patents/trademarks plus shareholdings in Total Carbide Ltd and Gnanomat SL, described as still trading solvent.

File here as history until a buyer of the graphene kit files a new operating address.
