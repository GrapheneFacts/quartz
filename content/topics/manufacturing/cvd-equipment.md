## Site

CVD Equipment Corporation. **355 South Technology Drive, Central Islip, New York 11722.** Founded 1982 in directories. NASDAQ **CVV**.

## What it is

Designs and builds CVD / thermal process tools and gas systems used by *other* people to grow graphene, SiC, CNTs, TMDCs. Application lab, not a GrapheneBlack tote. File next to Graphenea’s tool-using film line, not next to Sixth Element’s GO tanks.
