## European Commission — Graphene Flagship

- Jan 2013: selected as an FET Flagship. Commission communications used a **€1 billion over 10 years** envelope figure. That was a *programme scale*, not a wire of €1bn on day one. [[timeline/2013]]
- Oct 2013 kickoff: ramp-up often cited as **€54 million EC / 30 months** for the first phase.
- Mandate included graphene **and related layered materials**.

Label: Well-supported as Commission programme text. Not a valuation of any AIM/ASX name.
