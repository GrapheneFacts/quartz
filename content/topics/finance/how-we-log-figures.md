**Not advice.** A figure on this site is a *quoted statement* plus a label.

## Prefer

1. A primary: SEDAR+/ASX/AIM notice, SEC/SEDAR filing, DOE/EC contract page, company ASX/TSX release PDF.
2. The **unit** next to the number (USD vs AUD vs CAD vs EUR; award vs revenue vs capacity tonne).
3. Date the statement was made.

## Treat as Preliminary

- Small-cell or coupon tests that mention cost or “path to $X/kWh.”
- Company-stated capacity (“N t/year graphene”) until a third party or a consistent production report exists. Grade still matters ([[reference/forms]] · [[reference/standards]]).

## Treat as Unsourced / forecast

- “The graphene market will be $Y billion in 20ZZ.” Different houses count different solids (GO, GNP, film, “graphene-enhanced product”).
- Price targets and “buy/hold/sell” notes from brokers or AI roundups.
- Screenshots of a ticker with no filing.

Those sit on [[logs/unsourced]] if they keep circulating as facts.

## Stock “movements”

This archive is not a tape. We log:

- listings and ticker changes (e.g. a rename),
- announced placements / raises / grants when there is a release,
- not “up 12% today.”

If a later filing retracts a number, the page should say so. It should not vanish.
