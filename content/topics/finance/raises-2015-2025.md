## 2016

- **27 May 2016 — Directa Plus AIM admission (DCTA).** Placing of 17,033,334 new shares at **75p**. Company RNS: about **£12.8 million** gross; market cap at placing price about **£33.2 million**. Well-supported as an AIM first-day notice.

## 2017

- **Aug 2017 — NanoXplore RTO onto TSX Venture (GRA).** Concurrent equity about **C$9.70 million** at C$0.45 (Mason Graphite / company AIF trail). Trading from **8 Sep 2017**. Well-supported as listing + concurrent financing.

## 2019

- **Jan 2019 — NanoXplore.** Bought-deal / private package in the AIF: common shares about **C$21.0 million** at C$1.30 plus convertible debentures in the same package (gross share leg **C$20,988,240** in the French AIF text). Well-supported as AIF financing activity.

## 2021

- **12 Feb 2021 — NanoXplore.** Bought deal **11,500,000** shares at **C$4.00** for **C$46,000,000** gross. Well-supported AIF.
- **15 Jul 2021 — NanoXplore** graduates TSX-V → TSX. Listing change, not new cash.
- **Jan 2021 — Applied Graphene Materials.** Company later said it raised **£6 million** that month. Well-supported as AGM's own later circular language.
- **15 Apr 2021 — GMG** lists TSXV **GMG** via qualifying transaction with Cuspis; company letter cited a recent private placement of about **C$2 million** around listing. Well-supported as listing; placement size is company letter.
- **2 Sep 2021 — GMG.** Overnight marketed offering closed about **C$11.55 million** gross (5,635,000 units at C$2.05). Well-supported as closing release.
