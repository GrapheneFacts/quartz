**Evidence:** Mixed by row. Nothing here is medical, investment, or legal advice.

## Honest near-term buckets

- **Composites and coatings** — mechanical, barrier, corrosion, EMI, friction. Example order: [[topics/industry/2026-09-10-first-graphene-pacific-urethanes]]. Example test: Sparc/HydroGraph in [[weekly/2026-W38]].
- **Conductive additives** — batteries, plastics, inks. Ask loading and whether graphite would have done.
- **Thermal interface / heat spreaders** — film or platelet pads; interfaces dominate.
- **Membranes and barriers** — water, ions, gases; hole size and defects decide.
- **Sensors** — huge literature; few fielded instruments. Stencil-on-gold is a *fabrication* step toward sensors ([[posts/holey-graphene-molecular-stencil]]), not a cleared neural implant.
- **RF / photonics research devices** — real GHz papers ([[timeline/2010]], [[timeline/2011]]); not a phone SoC.

## Slow or stalled as originally sold

- **Replace silicon CMOS** — band gap and foundry economics ([[logs/theories]], [[timeline/2012]]).
- **Replace ITO tomorrow** — large-film demos exist ([[timeline/2010]]); qualification is the rest of the work.
- **Graphene battery as a category** — always unpack (additive vs anode vs coating). GMG 10C small cells: [[topics/industry/2026-09-09-gmg-battery-cycling]] (Preliminary).

## Biomedical

Large paper count (electrodes, scaffolds, drug-delivery vehicles, GO adjuvants in *animals*). Licensed human products are a different evidence class. No treatment claims on this site. Fringe overlay on vaccines is logged separately ([[logs/conspiracies]]).
