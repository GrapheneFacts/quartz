## What actually leaves a plant

Tonnes leave as powder, paste, masterbatch, or GO. Square metres leave as film or coating. An appliance is downstream.

- **Composites / elastomers** — wear, barrier, fire/char at low wt%. [[topics/manufacturing/pacific-urethanes|Pacific Urethanes]] + PureGRAPH into mining PU (FGR production PO, 9 Sep 2026). Cementene-class concrete = Versarien language, historical.
- **Coatings / inks** — corrosion, HVAC coil rebuild, EMI, conductive ink. GMG THERMAL-XR (company salt-spray talk). Vorbeck Vor-ink. Universal Matter Genable / asphalt notes.
- **Energy-storage additives** — conductive carbon in a slurry. Ask loading vs acetylene black. [[topics/manufacturing/conovate|eCOphite]] is graphene *monoxide*, not GO. GMG 10C small cells stay Preliminary. [[topics/industry/2026-09-09-gmg-battery-cycling]]
- **Films / heaters** — CVD or R2R → touch pilots, EMI, resistive **heater mats/tapes/panels**. [[topics/manufacturing/charmgraphene]] · [[topics/manufacturing/2d-carbon]] · Haydale JustHeat language. Not a Manchester toaster SKU.
- **Media / devices** — [[topics/manufacturing/zentek|ZenGUARD]] on mask or HVAC media. INBRAIN is an investigational electrode, not a mill product.
- **Membranes** — Nair 2012 / Abraham 2017 GO laminates. Lab and pilot language, not a municipal plant on this shelf.
- **Sensors / RF / photonics** — huge paper count; 2010–14 GHz and PD papers are real lab parts. Stencil-on-gold is a fabrication step. [[posts/holey-graphene-molecular-stencil]] · [[timeline/2011]]
- **Tools** — [[topics/manufacturing/cvd-equipment]] sells furnaces.
