## Language

- Graphite = graphene. No. [[reference/forms]]
- GO = graphene. No.
- A nanoplatelet tonne = a monolayer wafer. No.
- Wallace or Boehm “discovered graphene” in the 2004 sense. Too coarse. [[timeline/1947]] · [[timeline/1962]] · [[timeline/2004]]

## Physics-as-product

- Ideal monolayer tensile strength applies to a bulk composite at face value. No. [[reference/properties]]
- f_T of 100–155 GHz means graphene CPUs this decade. No. [[timeline/2011]]
- Flagship €1bn was a 2013 bank deposit. No. [[timeline/2013]]
