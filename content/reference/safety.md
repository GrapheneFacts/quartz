**Evidence:** Mixed. Field hygiene data can be well-supported. Many cell-culture papers are preliminary. Nothing here is medical advice.

## What is actually measured at work

NIOSH sampled workers at US graphene producers/users. Against a conservative **10 µg/m³** occupational *band* (not an OSHA PEL), many samples exceeded it when **dry powder** was handled. Advice in that literature: prefer masterbatch or liquid. [[topics/policy/2026-09-16-niosh-graphene-exposure]] · [[weekly/2026-W38]]

No disease cluster is attached to those particular notes on this site. Absence of a reported cluster is not a proof of lifelong safety.

## What the paper pile is

Thousands of papers on GO / graphene in cells, rodents, environmental models. Results depend on form, oxidation, metals, dose, and route (lung vs gut vs skin vs IV). That heterogeneity is why a single “graphene is toxic / graphene is safe” sentence is propaganda either direction.

## What this is not

- Proof that licensed vaccines contain GO ([[logs/conspiracies]]).
- Proof that a composite part sheds monolayer graphene into blood.
- A substitute for a site-specific industrial-hygiene plan.
