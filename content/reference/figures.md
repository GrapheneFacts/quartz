## CVD film after the copper is etched

![CVD graphene electrode](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Graphene.jpg?width=800)

Single-layer film grown on copper foil, then the copper etched away. The clear patch in the middle is the sheet. Author measured about 400 ohms sheet resistance.

Source: [File:Graphene.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Graphene.jpg). Author: Tavo Romann, 20 May 2014. License: CC BY-SA 4.0. Related paper: J. Power Sources, doi 10.1016/j.jpowsour.2015.01.153.
